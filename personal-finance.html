What is personal finance?

Personal finance can refer to different types of borrowing available to individuals for a range of personal purposes. The finance available to you will depend on your circumstances and the criteria of the relevant provider.

Before taking out finance, it is important to understand the interest rate, fees, repayment period and total amount that you may have to repay.

Important: Borrowing money costs more than the amount originally borrowed in many circumstances. Always consider affordability and the total cost before proceeding.

Types of personal finance

Different forms of finance may be available depending on your circumstances. These can include:

  • Personal loans
  • Debt consolidation finance
  • Secured finance
  • Other forms of personal borrowing

What should you consider before borrowing?

Before making a finance application or enquiry, consider whether the borrowing is affordable and whether it is appropriate for your circumstances.

  • The amount you want to borrow
  • The interest rate
  • The monthly repayment
  • The length of the agreement
  • Arrangement or other fees
  • The total amount repayable
  • Whether the repayments remain affordable

Can personal finance be used for different purposes?

Depending on the product and provider, personal finance may be considered for a variety of legitimate personal purposes.

The permitted use of funds, borrowing limits and eligibility requirements will vary between providers.

Personal finance and your circumstances

A finance provider may consider information such as income, affordability, existing commitments and credit history when assessing an application.

Meeting general criteria does not guarantee that finance will be offered. Each provider has its own eligibility requirements.

Can I make a personal finance enquiry?

Yes. You can make an enquiry through Finance Options UK and indicate that you are interested in personal finance.

With your permission, your enquiry can be passed to a relevant finance broker or provider for further discussion.

Is personal finance guaranteed?

No. Making an enquiry does not guarantee that finance will be available or that an application will be approved.

Any finance offered will be subject to the relevant provider's eligibility criteria, terms and conditions.
