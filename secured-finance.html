What is secured finance?

Secured finance is a type of borrowing where the finance is secured against an eligible asset, commonly property. Because the borrowing is secured, the relevant provider will consider the asset as part of its assessment.

Secured finance can be used for different purposes depending on the product, provider and the applicant's circumstances. The amount available, interest rate, fees and repayment arrangements can vary considerably.

Important: Making an enquiry does not guarantee that finance will be available or that an application will be approved.

How does secured finance work?

With secured borrowing, the finance provider has security over an eligible asset. The provider will normally assess factors such as the property or asset, the amount requested, affordability and its own lending criteria.

If finance is offered, the terms will depend on the relevant provider and your individual circumstances.

What can secured finance be used for?

Depending on the product and provider, secured finance may be considered for a range of purposes.

  • Property-related borrowing
  • Debt consolidation
  • Home improvements
  • Business or investment purposes
  • Other purposes accepted by the relevant provider

What should you consider before taking secured finance?

Secured borrowing is an important financial commitment. Before proceeding, you should carefully consider whether the finance is appropriate for your circumstances.

  • The total amount you will repay
  • Interest rates and fees
  • The length of the agreement
  • Whether you can afford the repayments
  • The terms and conditions of the finance
  • The consequences of failing to maintain repayments

Can I enquire about secured finance?

Yes. You can make an enquiry through Finance Options UK and provide information about the type of finance you are interested in.

With your permission, your enquiry can be passed to the relevant finance broker or provider for further discussion. Any decision about whether finance is available will be made by the relevant provider.
