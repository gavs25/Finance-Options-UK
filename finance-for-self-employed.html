Can self-employed people get finance?

Self-employed people may be able to access a range of finance products depending on their individual circumstances.

Being self-employed does not automatically mean that finance is unavailable. However, lenders and finance providers may assess income and affordability differently depending on how you earn your money.

Approval is not guaranteed and every provider has its own eligibility requirements.

What information may be required?

A finance provider may ask for information that helps it understand your financial circumstances and ability to afford repayments.

  • Details of your income
  • Information about your business or self-employment
  • Bank statements or other financial information
  • Tax or accounting information where appropriate
  • Existing financial commitments
  • Credit history

Finance options for self-employed applicants

Personal finance

Some self-employed applicants may be able to consider personal finance, subject to the provider's eligibility and affordability assessment.

Vehicle finance

Self-employed people may also consider vehicle finance. Available products and terms depend on individual circumstances and the finance provider.

Business finance

Business finance is designed for business-related purposes and can have different eligibility requirements from personal borrowing.

Self-employed with a limited company

Directors of limited companies may have different income structures from employees. For example, income can involve salary, dividends or other sources.

Finance providers may therefore request additional information to understand the applicant's overall financial position.

Self-employed with a poor credit history

Having both self-employed income and a poor credit history can make finding suitable finance more difficult, but different providers may have different eligibility criteria.

It is important to consider affordability carefully and avoid applying for finance that you cannot comfortably repay.

Important: No finance provider can guarantee that an applicant will be accepted. Any application is subject to the relevant provider's checks and criteria.

What should you consider before applying?

  • Whether the repayments are affordable
  • The total amount repayable
  • The interest rate and any applicable fees
  • The length of the finance agreement
  • Your existing debts and financial commitments
  • Whether borrowing is appropriate for your circumstances
